# Glasshouse at Dawn

Source Notes:
A humid public greenhouse opened just before sunrise, with condensation beading on the iron frames and glass panes. Garden staff moved slowly between rows of citrus tubs while early visitors paused near the fern hall to warm their hands around paper cups.

Key Observations:
Low winter light diffused through the fogged roof panels, making the tiled walkways shine softly. Damp soil, wet stone edging, and pooled water under benches created layered reflections that shifted as people passed.

Reusable Concepts:
condensation on glass panes
soft reflected dawn light
slow visitor movement through humid air

Uncertainties / Contradictions:
Some notes describe the room as nearly silent, while others mention frequent cart wheels and low conversation near the entrance.
