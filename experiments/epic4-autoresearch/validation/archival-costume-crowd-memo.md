# Archival Costume Crowd Memo

Source Notes:
Late-evening theater crowds exiting a riverfront picture house in October wore heavier coats than the afternoon queue, with felt hats and narrow gloves appearing most often near the marquee edge. Ushers and ticket takers stood under a wash of yellow bulbs while damp stone steps reflected the foot traffic.

Key Observations:
The scene reads as post-performance rather than casual daytime shopping. Small knots of patrons lingered near the poster cases, shoulders raised against the cold, while cigarette smoke and mist softened the street lamps.

Reusable Concepts:
felt hats and dark wool outerwear
yellow marquee spill on wet stone
lingering clusters of patrons after the show

Uncertainties / Contradictions:
Some descriptions lean formal and polished, while others stress practical rainwear and hurried movement.
