# Canyon Foundry Surface Sheet

Material Notes:
Cast iron cooling vats carry a bluish oxide bloom where rinse water evaporates unevenly. Sandstone dust collects along bolt recesses and softens the edge contrast on painted housings.

Surface Response:
Grease-darkened handles read nearly black until raking light catches a satin highlight across the worn metal. Powder-coated panels show chalking and faint abrasion where crates scrape the corners.

Wear And Damage:
Repeated tool contact rounds the paint off fastener heads, exposing bright nicked steel beneath the orange finish. Rubber seals harden and crack first on the sun-facing side of the assembly.

Environmental Effects:
Wind-driven grit settles into seams, and heat cycling lifts thin flakes of paint near the furnace doors.
