# Modular Enemy Generator Notes

Framework:
Build each enemy from one role, one mobility profile, one silhouette anchor, and one failure mode. Treat the role as mandatory and the others as adjustable.

Generation Rules:
If the role suggests ranged pressure, then keep the silhouette readable at distance and reduce close-combat clutter. If the failure mode is panic-prone, then animation and audio should expose instability early.

Template Example:
Role: scavenger sniper
Mobility profile: rooftop hopping
Silhouette anchor: crooked antenna rig
Failure mode: overcommits after losing cover

Contradiction Avoidance:
Do not combine heavy shield behavior with fragile skittish reactions unless the design is explicitly meant to feel unstable or improvised.
