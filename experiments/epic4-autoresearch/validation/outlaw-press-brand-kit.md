# Outlaw Press Brand Kit

Overview:
The publication should feel like a collision between regional crime folklore, pulp newspaper swagger, and deadpan contemporary satire. It needs enough visual grit to feel printed in a hurry, but enough strategic clarity that the audience understands the joke is intentional.

Reference Points:
Think tabloid urgency, desert-noir poster design, local scandal sheets, and a visual language built from bad halftones, blunt headlines, and crooked photography.

Evaluation Criteria:
Names and visual directions should be judged on tonal precision, memorability, extensibility across covers and social posts, and whether they can hold both menace and humor at once.

Format Implications:
The system should support recurring section brands, fake correction notices, lurid pull quotes, and a family of cover treatments that all feel part of the same publication universe.
